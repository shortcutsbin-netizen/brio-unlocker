# V64 full test procedure — one planned match, maximum two

The standing limit is **at most two played matches per test suite**. This suite requests **one natural match**. A second is optional only if a specific missed check needs it; do not play repeated matches to fill coverage. Unencountered conditions remain untested. Keep at most three distinct extra manual action types per match; repeated settings edits count as one, and export is separate.

1. **Load V64 on the game home screen.** Open `dist/brio-v64.min.js` in this repository, copy its complete raw contents, and paste/run it once in the browser Console using the established workflow. Confirm the terminal says **BRIO v64**. Open Extras → Modifiers and click **Set up normal test**, then **Set up V64 test**. This turns gameplay hide challenges off, enables supported modifiers and custom crosshair, and keeps existing passive bot/container collection focused. The saved-settings backup is retained. No asset retrieval or separate probe action is needed.

2. **Check crosshair color in this match.** Start ordinary Play. When convenient, open Extras → Modifiers → Enhanced/custom crosshair. Select a clearly nonwhite color, such as green or red. Close Extras and confirm that the aiming reticle uses it. Select a second nonwhite color and confirm it changes again. Use any Cross/Dot/Ring shape you prefer; the already-reported shape/size behavior does not need an exhaustive repeat. Uploaded image artwork keeps its own colors and is not tinted by the preset color control, so use a built-in shape for this check. Do not upload another image just for this suite.

3. **Check flashlight with Player follow.** Open Extras → Challenges, enable Flashlight mode, select **Player** follow and a comfortable radius, then close Extras. Walk/turn normally. Outside the clear circle, the world should darken while your own health/shield, inventory/item bar and ammo remain readable above the darkness. Native status text that naturally appears—storm countdown/announcements, airdrop or meteor notices, elimination messages—should also remain readable. Extras and terminal controls must remain usable. Do not wait for every announcement or hunt for an event.

4. **Check Cursor follow in the same match.** Change Flashlight follow to **Cursor**, close Extras and move the cursor over the game. The clear area should follow it, and your HUD and any naturally appearing status messages should stay above the dark effect even when outside the circle. Turn flashlight off once and confirm the view clears, then turn it back on and keep either follow mode enabled for match end. Leave gameplay hide challenges and Hell off for these visibility checks; their deliberate HUD hiding still takes precedence.

5. **Finish normal play and observe the end.** Play normally with available weapons and containers; there is no gun, rarity, opening, kill or win quota. The existing cone and other built modifiers need only exception reports if something visibly goes wrong. At a naturally reached elimination or win, flashlight darkness should ease away over about **0.3 seconds**, leaving the end screen clear. No special match-breakdown cycle or second match is required. If a failure makes play impractical, stop and export immediately instead of repeating matches.

6. **Export and report.** Click **COPY RESULTS** and upload the complete output as **`logs/runs/v64-log.txt`**. Preserve `v62-log.txt` and the current genuine `v63-log.txt`. Report: whether both nonwhite preset colors worked; whether health/inventory/ammo and naturally encountered messages stayed readable in **Player** and **Cursor** modes; whether flashlight off cleared the view; and whether match end faded smoothly. Name any missing HUD/message type and follow mode. Mark skipped/unencountered checks untested. A screenshot or short clip is useful if a visual fails, but no extra match is required to obtain one. After export, **Restore saved settings** is available if you want your pre-test preferences back.

Bot labels and exact unopened-container contents remain unresolved and are not expected to appear. Passive evidence is collected automatically; no additional broad recon run, asset pass or guessed human/bot label is requested.
