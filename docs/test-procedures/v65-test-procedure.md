# V65 full test procedure — one planned match, maximum two

**One natural match is requested. The hard maximum remains two played matches per suite.** Do not add matches to fill missed conditions. Flashlight is finalized and outlines are approved; neither needs a dedicated match or repeat control cycle. Bot identification and exact unopened-container screening are retired from this pipeline and have no test quota.

1. **Load V65 on the home screen.** Open `dist/brio-v65.min.js` in the repository, copy the complete raw file, and run it once in the browser Console using the established workflow. Confirm **BRIO v65** in the terminal. In Extras → Modifiers, click **Set up normal test**, then **Set up V65 test**. This enables the cone, keeps Hell and flashlight off for an unobstructed review, and preserves the existing settings backup. The unsupported bot/content controls should be absent. No asset retrieval or separate probe action is needed.

2. **Review the cone with any naturally obtained supported gun.** Walk and turn without firing, stop, fire normally, then return to walking. Within the same gun and hip/ADS mode, the cone should keep a fixed shape and a fixed aimed-gun reference relative to the player. It should move with the player and rotate with aim, without swinging with the walking hand, recoiling, orbiting the player, or repeatedly opening/narrowing while firing. Its centerline should point toward your native aim/cursor. Check the origin visually: it should sit at the stable forward gun reference, rather than behind or beside the player. Exact barrel-art alignment is the main remaining visual question; do not assume it is correct merely because the motion is stable.

3. **Compare hip and ADS in that same match.** ADS and release normally with the same gun. Each gun/mode has its own fixed empirical width, so a deliberate hip↔ADS change may change width; it must not keep breathing within either mode. The position reference stays fixed rather than following the lowered/raised hand. A naturally encountered weapon switch may select a different fixed reference and width. Camera zoom and your explicit cone-size preference scale the visual normally. No all-gun, clip, rarity or kill quota is requested.

4. **Continue normal play or stop early on a clear failure.** Other supported modifiers need exception reports only. Do not repeat flashlight, outlines, old challenge cycles, custom-image upload or container-opening tests. If convenient, note whether your saved crosshair color looks correct; no separate color cycle or match is needed. If the cone still looks wrong, stop spending matches on it: one screenshot or short clip showing the player, gun, cursor, cone and the failing walking/firing/ADS state is more useful than another long numeric-only run. No extra match is required to obtain that evidence. Keep at most three distinct extra manual action types per match; repeated settings edits count as one and export is separate.

5. **Export and report.** Click **COPY RESULTS** after the natural match end, or earlier if a clear failure occurs. Upload the entire export as **`logs/runs/v65-log.txt`** and preserve earlier logs. Report the gun and mode, whether the cone stayed rigid, whether its centerline followed aim, and whether its origin looked correctly placed. Include the visual evidence if it failed and available; mark skipped/unencountered conditions untested. Optionally use **Restore saved settings** after export. A second match is optional only for a specific unresolved check and is never required for routine coverage.

No bot labels or exact pre-open loot labels are expected. Native storm/shot/match-end observations and supported-feature diagnostics remain automatic; retired broad bot/container snapshots are not being requested again. If the cone still fails without usable visual evidence, the next step is to resolve that evidence gap before another geometry correction.
