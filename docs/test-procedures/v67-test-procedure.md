# V67 final operational test — maximum two played matches total

Use two matches for this final review: one normal modifier match and one Hell match. Do not replay matches for missed events. Stop early and export if a failure makes play impractical. Previously finalized features need exception reports only; no spread, bot/content screening, container, gun, kill or win quotas.

1. **Load V67 on home.** Open `dist/brio-v67.min.js`, copy the entire raw file and run it once in the browser Console using the established workflow. Confirm **BRIO v67**. In Extras → Modifiers, click **Set up V67 modifiers**. This selects normal supported modifiers, enables the projected storm highlight, remote-player halo and custom crosshair, and disables gameplay challenges. Your original settings backup is retained. “No storm countdown” should be absent; the old “Hide info popups” row is now **Hide interaction prompts**. Spread and bot/content screening stay absent.

2. **Match 1 — normal modifier review.** Play normally. When a next storm zone is announced, compare the cyan boundary in the world with the next-zone square on the minimap. It should mark the projected NEXT zone, remain tied to that target while the live storm moves, and form a complete square: top, bottom, left and right, with a stronger cyan line and dark backing. Each side is naturally visible only when that side is within your camera view; do not travel into danger or add matches just to see every side. If convenient, toggle Storm edge highlight off/on once to check restoration. Report any mismatch, absent on-screen side or poor line visibility, preferably with a screenshot showing world and minimap together.

3. **During that same normal match, give the remaining surfaces a final natural-use glance.** Check the yellow remote-player halo; custom crosshair color; remote inventory slots/material spacing; safe-zone edge and center arrows; chosen gold/red loot arrows; matching owned-ammo outlines; and past projectile trails when they naturally appear. No item-by-item hunt is needed. Existing local PNG/WebP crosshair upload is optional; otherwise mark upload untested. At the natural match end, check Match breakdown for readable values, agreement with native placement/eliminations/damage, and duplicate/missing panels. Its visual redesign is intentionally deferred. Explicitly say which pending surfaces you observed working; do not infer a pass for skipped conditions.

4. **Match 2 — Hell and always-visible native messages.** Return home and click **Set up V67 challenges (Hell)**. This selects highest-tier gameplay challenges and disables modifiers; flashlight and monochrome stay off for readability. During lobby/play, confirm native waiting/storm/event messages, elimination banners, kill feed and storm countdown remain visible when naturally produced. The minimap and player/kill counters should still hide. Bodies/held art, loot/builds, own inventory/health HUD, crosshair, storm shading, chests, pickup/interaction prompts, damage/hit/progress cues and projectiles should hide within their documented scopes. Report a visible leak or unrelated disappearance. If practical, briefly turn Hell off and enable **Hide interaction prompts** alone: build/inventory/pickup instructions should hide while status messages and kill feed remain. This is an optional settings check within the same match, not a reason for another match. You may stop early if Hell is impractical.

5. **Export the entire run once.** After both matches, or at an early failure, click **COPY RESULTS** and upload the complete output as **`logs/runs/v67-log.txt`**, preserving earlier logs. V67 automatically emits **V67 COMPLETION AUDIT** at native match end and export, with all 49 retained options, enabled states, reached/suppressed contexts, pending approvals and the always-visible policy. No separate probe/PERF/manual match-start/auto-cycle action is needed. In chat, report projected-zone alignment/line visibility, status/kill-feed/countdown visibility in Hell, any challenge leak, Match breakdown appearance and which other pending surfaces you explicitly checked. Mark missed conditions untested. Keep at most three distinct extra manual action types per match; settings edits count as one and export is separate. Use **Restore saved settings** after export if desired.

This suite is the final operational review candidate. A clean log plus your explicit acceptance can close operational development; unobserved contexts remain documented limits. Production menu/end-screen redesign, in-game terminal removal and memory optimization across matches are next-phase work, not part of V67. No extra matches are required for lower tiers or events that do not occur.
