# Cloud access and gameplay questionnaire

Status: awaiting user answers. No cloud session opened. Short numbered answers are sufficient; unknown/skip is acceptable. Existing confirmed behavior need not be re-explained.

1. Account/sign-in: will you use a dedicated test Google/game account or your usual account? Can you complete a supported interactive sign-in handoff when prompted? Do not send passwords, MFA codes, cookies, or tokens in chat/repo. Can we change its display name to unlocker_test_observer, and are there any account actions/settings to avoid? Purchases, currency spending, and account linking/deletion are outside the trial.
2. Connection and setup: which server region, desktop game settings, browser zoom/resolution (if important), and mode should we use? Are there loading/ad/consent screens, fullscreen requirements, or connection issues we should know about?
3. Controls: are bindings default? List movement, interact/open/pickup, reload, inventory slots/cycling, drop items, healing/consumable use, map, and build entry/place/rotate/material selection. ADS is already known to be held right-click; mention toggle/remapping exceptions. A settings screenshot or copied bindings is fine.
4. Match start: what steps take us from Play to controllable gameplay? Is spawn/drop selection available? What is the easiest low-pressure way to obtain a gun and reach an openable object for a first test?
5. Interaction details: does pickup/opening require a tap, hold, proximity, or aiming? Any special behavior for chests, legendary chests, ammo/grenade crates, fishing, and airdrops? Mention only important distinctions and common failure conditions.
6. Survival and resets: how do healing/shields, storm timing, death, spectating, and return-to-home work? Can a dead player spectate a chosen teammate/opponent? Are private/practice/custom matches available, and do they preserve the mechanics we need to test?
7. Party procedure: describe create/join by ID, choose duos/squads, ready/start, auto-fill controls, and how to confirm both clients share the same match. Does one Google/game account work concurrently in two tabs without disconnecting either, or does the decoy need a guest/separate account? Do names/settings share across tabs?
8. Coordinated testing: what is the best way to coordinate while you watch the cloud screen? Any practical queue countdown, meeting location, or constraints? When a test needs your help, should I pause and request a joint round, or complete independent checks first? No schedule commitment is needed now.
9. Cosmetics: which representative owned/available cosmetic items should we use to confirm authenticated access and script behavior? Any known entitlement, equip/persistence, or account-state pitfalls? No full collection list required.
10. Initial acceptance: after access/sign-in, I propose one basic movement + hip/ADS firing + one opening test, then log/output retrieval, before any code iteration. Is there a different priority or known gameplay behavior that would make this sequence misleading?
11. Remaining pitfalls: are there other important mechanics, cooldowns, penalties, disconnect/idle behavior, game rules, or limitations of the two-tab method that could invalidate observations or make an unattended decoy unsuitable?

Recommended before answers: follow ../supplemental/asset-retrieval.md for two natural matches, upload reviewed flat results to docs/game-sources, and report any collector errors. This helps subsequent source/geometry analysis; it is not a prerequisite imposed by the browser itself. Further questions may follow the evidence.
